- The entire website must be in Arabic.

- Soft magical atmosphere.
- Very smooth animations.
- No distracting elements.

At the center, show a beautiful glowing sun/moon-like celestial object representing "Shams", with a warm golden glow surrounded by subtle light particles.

The countdown should display:
- الأيام
- الساعات

- الثواني

Above or below the countdown, display an elegant Arabic message such as:

"بقي القليل على يومكِ يا شمس..."

The design should create a feeling of anticipation and mystery.

When the countdown reaches exactly September 12 at 12:00 AM Iraq time:

- The countdown disappears smoothly.
- The screen becomes brighter.
- The stars become more visible and start gently moving.
- The central glowing celestial object becomes brighter.
- Add a beautiful magical light animation.

Then reveal the main birthday message:

"كل عام وأنتِ بخير يا شمس ❤️"

Make this moment feel special and emotional, like the website has been waiting for her birthday.

Do NOT automatically reveal everything at once.

The website must be designed primarily for mobile phones.

Requirements:
- Fully responsive.
- Arabic RTL layout.
- Large readable Arabic text.
- Touch-friendly buttons.
- No horizontal scrolling.
- Fast loading.
- Animations should not cause lag.
- The experience should look beautiful on a phone screen.

## IMPORTANT TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

- Use real Iraq time (Asia/Baghdad, UTC+3) for the countdown.
- The countdown must automatically detect whether the birthday has started.
- Before September 12 at 12:00 AM Iraq time → show countdown.
- At/after September 12 at 12:00 AM Iraq time → show the birthday experience.
- Do not rely only on the user's device clock if a reliable timezone conversion can be used.
- Store the birthday date clearly in the code so it can be easily changed later.
- Make the experience work without requiring login or registration.
- Do not add ads.
- Do not add unnecessary navigation.
- The website should feel like one continuous interactive birthday experience.
